Morning (most popular)

Taking shilajit in the morning fits its traditional use as an energy tonic. Dissolve it in warm water, tea or your morning coffee. It is also the easiest time to make it a daily habit.

Before a workout

Active people often take it 30 to 60 minutes before training. If you train in the morning, this combines nicely with your normal routine. More in shilajit for athletes.

If you are building a morning or pre-workout routine and still need a jar, our current pick is Pure Himalayan Shilajit.

Evening

Some users take a smaller dose in the evening and sleep fine, but others feel too alert. If you try it at night, take it at least a few hours before bed and see how you sleep.

With or without food?

An empty stomach is common, but if you feel any nausea, take it with breakfast. Both are fine.

The routine that works

  • Same time every day
  • Pea-sized amount in a warm drink
  • At least 8 weeks before judging results

Frequently asked questions

Should I take shilajit on an empty stomach?

Many people take it on an empty stomach in the morning. If it upsets your stomach, take it with breakfast instead.

Can I take shilajit at night?

Some people do, but because it is associated with energy, it may affect sleep for others. Morning is the safer default.
