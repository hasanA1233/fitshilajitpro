Why women use shilajit

  • Daily energy: the most common reason, especially during busy periods.
  • Minerals: shilajit naturally contains iron and other trace minerals in small amounts.
  • Training: active women add it to their workout routine.

Research specifically in women is limited, so think of shilajit as a traditional supplement rather than a treatment.

How women can take it

The routine is the same as for anyone: a pea-sized amount of resin dissolved in a warm drink once a day, usually in the morning. Some women start with half a serving. Our how-to guide explains each step.

If you want to try it, our current pick is Pure Himalayan Shilajit. Consider starting with half a serving, and read the safety points below first.

Important safety points

  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding: do not use shilajit.
  • Iron: if you have high iron levels or hemochromatosis, avoid it unless your doctor says otherwise.
  • Medication: check with your doctor if you take any regular medication.
  • Quality: only use purified, lab-tested resin. Learn how to spot fakes.

Resin or tablets?

Women who dislike the earthy taste often prefer tablets. Compare the options in resin vs capsules vs powder.

Frequently asked questions

Is shilajit safe for women?

Many women take it without problems at the label dose. Avoid it during pregnancy and breastfeeding, and check with a doctor if you have a medical condition.

Does shilajit affect hormones in women?

There is very little research on this in women, so avoid strong claims and talk to a doctor if you have a hormone-related condition.
