Resin

Pros: closest to the traditional form, easy to test for authenticity (see our 6 tests), flexible dose. Cons: sticky, strong taste, takes a minute to prepare.

Tablets and capsules

Pros: no taste, pre-measured, perfect for travel. Cons: harder to verify at home, may contain binders. Choose brands with lab-tested tablets.

If resin sounds right for you, our current pick is Pure Himalayan Shilajit.

Powder

Pros: mixes into smoothies. Cons: easy to adulterate and hard to dose without a small scale. Many experts consider it the riskiest form to buy.

Liquid drops

Pros: very convenient. Cons: the concentration varies a lot between brands and is hard to check.

Our verdict

Start with resin if you want the most traditional, verifiable option. Choose tablets if taste or convenience would stop you from taking it daily. Many people keep both: resin at home and tablets for travel.

Frequently asked questions

Is resin stronger than capsules?

Resin is the least processed form, so many people prefer it. Strength depends on the product and dose, so compare servings and lab results rather than form alone.

Which form is best for travel?

Tablets or capsules. They are pre-dosed, tasteless and do not melt in a warm bag.
