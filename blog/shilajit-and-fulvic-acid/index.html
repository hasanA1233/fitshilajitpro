What is fulvic acid?

Fulvic acid is a natural organic compound that forms when plant matter decomposes in soil. It is part of a group called humic substances. Shilajit is one of the most concentrated natural sources of it.

Why it is interesting

Fulvic acid is small and water-soluble. Researchers have studied how it binds minerals, and it is often described as helping carry minerals, though human evidence is still limited. It has also been studied for antioxidant properties in the lab.

Fulvic acid comes naturally with shilajit rather than as a separate extract. If you want to try it, our current pick is Pure Himalayan Shilajit.

Fulvic acid and trace minerals

Quality shilajit is often described as containing 85+ trace minerals in small amounts. Together with fulvic acid, this mineral profile is one of the main reasons people take shilajit as a daily supplement.

How to choose a fulvic-rich shilajit

  • Look for products that publish lab results showing fulvic acid content.
  • Prefer purified resin from a stated source.
  • Avoid products with vague labels and no testing.

Frequently asked questions

How much fulvic acid is in shilajit?

It varies by source and product. Quality brands often state a percentage on the label or in their lab results.

Is fulvic acid the same as shilajit?

No. Fulvic acid is one component of shilajit. The resin also contains humic substances, minerals and other compounds.
