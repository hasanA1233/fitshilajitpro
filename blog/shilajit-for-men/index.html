In traditional practice, shilajit was often described as a tonic for male strength and vitality. Today, men mostly use it for energy, training and healthy testosterone levels as they age.

What the testosterone study found

A randomized, placebo-controlled study published in Andrologia (2016) followed healthy men aged 45 to 55 who took purified shilajit twice a day for 90 days. Compared with placebo, the shilajit group had significantly higher total testosterone at the end of the study.

It is a promising result, but keep it in perspective: it was one small study, and testosterone naturally varies with sleep, stress, weight and age.

Energy and training

Many men add shilajit to a morning routine or take it before workouts. A small trial found better strength retention after fatiguing exercise in men taking a higher dose. Read more in shilajit for athletes.

If you want to try it, our current pick is Pure Himalayan Shilajit. Give it 8 to 12 weeks alongside good sleep and training before judging how it fits your routine.

A simple routine for men

  • Take a pea-sized amount of resin in warm water or coffee each morning.
  • Stay consistent for at least 8 to 12 weeks before judging results.
  • Support it with the basics: sleep, strength training, protein and less alcohol.

Who should be careful

  • Men with prostate or hormone-related conditions
  • Anyone taking medication, especially for blood pressure or diabetes
  • People with high iron levels (hemochromatosis)

In these cases, talk to your doctor first. See the full shilajit safety guide.

Frequently asked questions

Does shilajit increase testosterone?

A 2016 study in men aged 45 to 55 found higher total testosterone after 90 days of purified shilajit versus placebo. It was a small study, so results may vary.

Can men take shilajit every day?

Many men take it daily at the label dose. Speak to a doctor first if you have a medical condition or take medication.
