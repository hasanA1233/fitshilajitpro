How to Take Shilajit Resin: Dosage, Timing & Tips

Resin is the most traditional form of shilajit, and it is easy to use once you know the routine. Here is exactly how to do it.

Shilajit resin looks intimidating the first time you open the jar: it is dark, sticky and hard when cold. But taking it only takes about a minute a day.

How much to take

The usual serving is a pea-sized amount, often described as roughly 300 to 500 mg. Most jars come with a small measuring spoon. A 20 g jar gives around 65 servings, which is about two months of daily use.

In your first week, try half a serving to see how your body responds, then move to the full amount on the label.

Step by step

  1. Scoop a pea-sized amount with the spoon. If the resin is hard, warm the jar in your hand for a minute.
  2. Dissolve it in a cup of warm water, herbal tea or coffee. Stir for 20 to 30 seconds.
  3. Drink it. You can also let it dissolve under your tongue if you do not mind the taste.

If you do not have a jar yet, our current pick is Pure Himalayan Shilajit. The same steps apply to any resin.

When to take it

Most people take shilajit in the morning or before training, since it is associated with energy. Taking it late in the evening may not suit everyone. We go deeper in the best time to take shilajit.

Tips that make it easier

  • Taste: add a little honey or lemon to the drink, or mix it into coffee.
  • Consistency: link it to a daily habit, like your morning coffee.
  • Cycling: some people take short breaks every few months; follow your label or doctor's advice.

How to store it

Keep the lid tightly closed and store the jar in a cool, dry place away from sunlight. The resin naturally gets harder in cold weather and softer in heat; that is normal and actually a sign of real shilajit. Learn more in how to spot fake shilajit.

Do not take shilajit if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, and speak to your doctor first if you have a medical condition or take medication.

Frequently asked questions

How much shilajit resin should I take?

Most labels suggest a pea-sized portion (roughly 300 to 500 mg) once a day. Start lower in the first week and follow your product's label.

Can I put shilajit in hot coffee?

Yes, warm or hot drinks work well. Many people avoid boiling water and let the drink cool slightly first.
