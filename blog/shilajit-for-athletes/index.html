Shilajit for Athletes: Energy, Strength & Recovery

Updated November 19, 2026 · 3 min read

From lifters to runners, more active people are adding shilajit to their routine. Here is what to know.

In this article
  1. What the research suggests
  2. Why athletes use it
  3. How to use it around training
  4. Tablets for the gym bag
  5. Stay safe

Athletes look for natural ways to support energy and recovery, and shilajit has become a popular option.

What the research suggests

A small study in recreationally active men tested purified shilajit for eight weeks. Men taking the higher dose kept more of their maximal strength after a fatiguing exercise protocol than those on placebo. It is an encouraging early result rather than proof.

Why athletes use it

  • Energy for training sessions
  • Minerals lost through sweat (in small amounts)
  • Routine: a simple natural addition to a pre-workout habit

How to use it around training

  1. Take a pea-sized amount of resin in warm water 30 to 60 minutes before training, or with breakfast.
  2. On rest days, take it at the same time to stay consistent.
  3. Give it 8 to 12 weeks alongside good sleep and nutrition.

Tablets for the gym bag

If you train away from home, tablets are easier to carry and need no preparation. Compare options in resin vs capsules vs powder.

Stay safe

Choose lab-tested products, follow the label and check our safety guide. Competitive athletes should prefer products tested for banned substances.

Frequently asked questions

Is shilajit allowed in sport?

Shilajit itself is not a prohibited substance, but supplements can be contaminated. Competitive athletes should choose third-party tested products and check their federation's rules.

Should I take shilajit before or after training?

Most people take it 30 to 60 minutes before training or in the morning. Consistency matters more than exact timing.
